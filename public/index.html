How to use
  1. Upload a CSV or add a new order. Prepaid orders skip COD scoring.
  2. Review the highest-risk orders first. Each score uses buyer history, address quality and pin return rate. City is never a scoring signal. Value, phone frequency and verification flags are explainable inputs.
  3. LOW 0–29 suggests ship, requiring your approval. MEDIUM 30–49 asks for YES. HIGH 50–69 asks for ₹49, VERY HIGH 70–84 for ₹99, EXTREME 85+ for ₹149.
  4. Preview and copy the WhatsApp message, or open WhatsApp to send it yourself. Nothing is sent automatically.
  5. Record a simulated reply, then Ship, Hold or Cancel. S/H/C and arrow keys work outside form fields. Decisions auto-advance.
  6. After 12h, one mock reminder is generated. Silence updates the suggestion only. All actions need a human click except 24h silent upfront orders, which auto-cancel if the brand toggle is on.
Bulk actions affect only this sandbox. Payments and address checks are simulated. Connect a real provider only after production security and privacy work.
